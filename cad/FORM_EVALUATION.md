# Form Evaluation Protocol

## Participants and Setup

Test with the designer and 5-8 additional users. Present neutral 1:1 prints in randomized order.
Use only the rear dimple code during handling; do not reveal form names or CMF renders until the
neutral-form evaluation is complete.

## Tasks

1. Pick up and orient the form without looking at its control face.
2. Place it in a normal trouser, jacket, or bag pocket and retrieve it while walking.
3. Locate the button landmark and simulate wake, record, pause, and one-second hold actions.
4. Change grip, raise it for a short spoken note, and return it to the pocket.
5. Compare all ten forms and identify strongest and weakest attributes.
6. After form scoring, review the complete CMF matrix separately.

chosen CMF, reasons, rejected tradeoffs, and any dimensional revisions in the decision log below.
